- **Geopolitical Factors**: Sanctions imposed by Western nations due to Russia’s annexation of Crimea and involvement in Ukraine (2014).
- **Oil Price Collapse**: A sharp drop in global oil prices (Russia’s primary export) worsened the economic downturn.
- **Currency Devaluation**: The Russian ruble lost significant value, increasing inflation and reducing purchasing power.
- **Capital Flight**: Investors withdrew funds due to uncertainty, exacerbating economic instability.

---

### **2. Domestic Impact on Russia**
#### **Economic Consequences**
- **Recession**: GDP contracted by ~3.7% in 2015.
- **Inflation**: Spiked to ~16% in 2015 due to currency devaluation and import costs.
- **Unemployment**: Rose, though not as severely as in past crises.
- **Foreign Investment**: Plummeted by 92% by 2015; over 200 startups closed.
- **Sectoral Effects**:
  - **Energy**: Sanctions halted joint projects (e.g., ExxonMobil-Rosneft Arctic exploration).
  - **Manufacturing/Retail**: Consumer spending dropped (e.g., McDonald’s closed stores, Adidas suspended expansion).

#### **Demographic Consequences**
- **Poverty**: 23 million Russians lived below the poverty line (2015).
- **Emigration**: Over 2,000 millionaires left Russia; 330,000 applied for U.S. Green Cards (2016).
- **Health**: Increased mortality and declining fertility rates.

---

### **3. Global Financial Market Impact**
- **Stock Markets**: U.S. markets (e.g., Dow Jones) declined due to spillover effects.
- **Currency Volatility**: The ruble’s collapse disrupted forex trading (e.g., FXCM restricted USD/RUB trades).
- **Corporate Losses**:
  - **Energy**: BP lost 17% market share; Total S.A. shelved shale projects.
  - **Automotive**: Ford (-40% sales), Volkswagen (-20% sales).
  - **Food/Retail**: Danone, Carlsberg, and Adidas faced revenue losses.

---

### **4. Impact on Former Soviet Republics**
- **Currency Devaluations**:
  - **Ukraine**: Hryvnia lost 60% value (2014–2015).
  - **Belarus**: Rubel hit record lows.
  - **Kazakhstan**: Tenge devalued 40% (2015).
  - **Armenia**: Dram depreciated 15% (2015).
- **Remittances**: Migrant workers’ earnings dropped (e.g., Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan).
- **Trade Disruptions**: Reduced demand for exports from neighboring countries.

---

### **5. Long-Term Effects**
- **Sanctions Legacy**: Continued isolation from Western financial systems.
- **Economic Diversification**: Russia shifted focus to Asia (e.g., China) for trade and investment.
- **Eurasian Economic Union**: Strengthened regional economic ties post-crisis.
