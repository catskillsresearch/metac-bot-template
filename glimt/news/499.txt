**Kadyrov Enters Conflict with Kremlin Over Guarantees for Relatives**
Chechen leader Ramzan Kadyrov has entered into conflict with the Kremlin, with his relationships with Russian dictator Vladimir Putin deteriorating in recent months. According to sources, Kadyrov secretly sought guarantees for his relatives in Muslim countries in the Middle East, but the FSB immediately found out and reported it to Putin. The main reason for the conflict is the information from the FSB about Kadyrov's negotiations with representatives of Muslim monarchies in the Middle East regarding the future of his assets and the security of his family. These negotiations were not coordinated with the Kremlin. Kadyrov is reportedly seriously ill, with kidney and pancreas problems, and his condition is worsening. He has been trying to hide his health issues by being more active on social media, but he has been absent from public life for longer periods of time. Kadyrov knows the risks of losing power and the consequences for his family if he steps down, and he is seeking guarantees for them in Muslim countries. He has close relationships with the leaders of these countries and has been trying to hide these negotiations from the Kremlin. However, the FSB has found out and reported it to Putin. Kadyrov's daughter, Aishat, has been removed from her position as vice-premier of Chechnya, and her sister, Tabarik, has also been removed from the register of business owners. Both women are under US and Japanese sanctions. The conflict has also affected Kadyrov's closest ally, Adam Delimkhanov, who has refused to participate in any military conflicts since the beginning of 2025, citing a strict instruction from the top to reduce the visibility of Chechen leaders and security forces.
